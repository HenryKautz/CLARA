Each problem's evidence-free recognition theory — the problem at its recognition
horizon, with the goal literals as the hypotheses, as recognize.sh builds it —
compiled by marginals.sh --solver d4 --project. The projection keeps the drive
actions and the final-slice goal atoms, and FiFO first verifies that they
determine every other atom, so the projected circuit gives exact counts; any
pinned observations are unit literals over the kept actions, so one saved
circuit answers every evidence file by conditioning, without recompiling. CNF
variables and clauses are those of the ground theory d4 is given; projected
variables are the ones it keeps. d4 time is the d4 run alone; total time is the
whole marginals.sh run (reading the theory, the projection check, d4, importing
and saving the circuit, and the marginals of the unconditioned circuit). Circuit
nodes and edges are those of d4's decision-DNNF, before FiFO smooths it. Times
are wall-clock, 1 or 2 compilations at a time (grid8x8-2, grid8x8-3, grid8x8-4,
grid9x9-1, grid9x9-2, grid9x9-3, grid9x9-4 one at a time: two 8 GB SBCL imports
at once swap on this 16 GB machine), each limited to 30 minutes. SBCL ran with
an 8 GB heap (grid9x9-4: 12 GB: with 8 GB, SBCL's garbage collector failed while
importing its circuit) and a 1 GB control stack, d4 with a 64 MB stack (the
defaults, 1 GB, 2 MB and 8 MB, are too small from 7×7 or 9×9 on). The circuits
were imported by FiFO with its d4 importer rewritten to use bit-vector scopes
and iterative traversal (branch ddnnf-import-bitvector-scopes); the released
importer runs out of memory on circuits of about a million nodes. Every circuit
kept was checked: its model count (all literals set to 1 in the smoothed
circuit) equals the number of truck walks the theory allows, counted
independently by dynamic programming over the grid (H-1 steps, each a drive to a
neighbouring cell or a wait, from p1-1 to a goal cell), a count that agrees with
sharpSAT on every problem up to 7×7 (sharpSAT did not finish an 8×8 problem in
hours). The check is needed: from 8×8 on, d4 sometimes crashes (bus error or
segmentation fault) and sometimes returns a wrong circuit without any error, and
repeated runs on the same CNF give different circuits, so each problem was
compiled until a run passed. Tries counts the runs of d4 on a problem, failed
ones included, and the times are those of the run that passed. For grid9x9-4,
FiFO's circuit-size cap (*ddnnf-node-limit*, 2,000,000 nodes) was raised to
20,000,000: the smoothed import of its d4 circuit exceeds the default. In the
run reported, d4 was given extra options: "--cache-store-strategy all --cache-
size-first-page 268435456 --cache-size-additional-page 268435456" for grid8x8-2,
grid9x9-1, grid9x9-2, grid9x9-3, grid9x9-4; "--cache-store-strategy all" for
grid8x8-3 (every other problem: d4's defaults). Storing every clause in a cache
entry, and cache pages smaller than the default 4 GB, made d4 miscompile less
often, though not never. "error": no run finished — d4 crashed every time. A
timeout with circuit sizes: d4 finished, but importing and saving its circuit
did not within the limit. No 9×9 problem compiled within the limit, so larger
grids were not tried.
