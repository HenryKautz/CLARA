Each problem's evidence-free recognition theory — the problem at its recognition
horizon, with the goal literals as the hypotheses, as recognize.sh builds it —
compiled by marginals.sh --solver d4 --project. The projection keeps the drive
actions and the final-slice goal atoms, and FiFO first verifies that they
determine every other atom, so the projected circuit gives exact counts; any
pinned observations are unit literals over the kept actions, so one saved
circuit answers every evidence file by conditioning, without recompiling. CNF
variables and clauses are those of the ground theory d4 is given; projected
variables are the ones it keeps. d4 time is the d4 run alone; total time is the
whole marginals.sh run (reading the theory, the projection check, d4, importing
and saving the circuit, and the marginals of the unconditioned circuit). Circuit
nodes and edges are those of d4's decision-DNNF, before FiFO smooths it. Times
are wall-clock, 1 compilation at a time, except grid8x8-1 (2 at a time), each
limited to 30 minutes. SBCL ran with an 8 GB heap (grid9x9-4: 12 GB: with 8 GB,
SBCL's garbage collector failed while importing its circuit) and a 1 GB control
stack, d4 with a 64 MB stack (the defaults, 1 GB, 2 MB and 8 MB, are too small
for the larger circuits). The circuits were imported by FiFO's d4 importer as
rewritten to use bit-vector scopes and iterative traversal (FiFO fd0d0b9); the
earlier importer ran out of memory on circuits of about a million nodes. Model
count (verified) is the number of models of the compiled circuit (all literals
set to 1 in the smoothed circuit), that is, the number of truck walks the theory
allows. Every circuit kept was checked against an independent count of those
walks by dynamic programming over the grid (H-1 steps, each a drive to a
neighbouring cell or a wait, from p1-1 to a goal cell), which agrees with
sharpSAT on every problem up to 7×7 (sharpSAT did not finish an 8×8 problem in
2.5 hours); the two counts were equal for every circuit in the table. Grids past
7×7 were not re-run with the current FiFO: from 8×8 on, d4 often crashes (bus
error or segmentation fault) or returns a wrong circuit without any error, and
repeated runs on the same CNF give different circuits. Their rows give the run
whose circuit passed the model-count check (grid8x8-1: correct on 1 of 1;
grid8x8-2: correct on 1 of 8; grid8x8-3: correct on 1 of 10; grid8x8-4: correct
on 1 of 6 runs); its d4 time is that run's alone, not counting failed runs.
Their total time is not shown: it was measured with the earlier, quadratic
evaluator (and partly two compilations at a time); and no queries were timed on
them. Tries counts the runs of d4 on a problem, failed ones included — a run
whose saved circuit a later check found wrong counts as failed. Query with 2
obs. is the time to recompute every marginal of the saved circuit conditioned on
two observations — the first two drives of a shortest plan to the problem's
alphabetically first destination, pinned to steps 1 and 2 (evidence/PROBLEM-
DESTINATION-obs2.txt) — as marginals.sh --circuit --evidence-file does: the
observations are unit literals over kept atoms, so the circuit is clamped and
re-evaluated, not recompiled. It is timed inside SBCL, the median of 5
repetitions, and excludes starting SBCL and loading FiFO (about 1.2 s) and
reading the circuit file (0.001–0.45 s). Each answer was checked: both observed
drives have probability 1, and the final-slice goal marginals sum to 1. FiFO
evaluates the circuit in two linear passes; its backward pass through an AND
node of k children uses prefix and suffix products (an earlier version
recomputed each child's k-1 sibling products, k² multiplications, and took up to
7 s on 7×7 — smoothed AND nodes here have up to 1,007 children). For grid9x9-4,
FiFO's circuit-size cap (*ddnnf-node-limit*, 2,000,000 nodes) was raised to
20,000,000: the smoothed import of its d4 circuit exceeds the default. In the
run reported, d4 was given extra options: "--cache-store-strategy all --cache-
size-first-page 268435456 --cache-size-additional-page 268435456" for grid8x8-2,
grid9x9-1, grid9x9-2, grid9x9-3, grid9x9-4; "--cache-store-strategy all" for
grid8x8-3 (every other problem: d4's defaults). Storing every clause in a cache
entry, and cache pages smaller than the default 4 GB, made d4 miscompile less
often, though not never. "crashed at": no run finished — d4 crashed every time;
the time is when it crashed in the last run. A timeout with circuit sizes: d4
finished, but importing and saving its circuit did not within the limit. No 9×9
problem compiled within the limit, so larger grids were not tried.
