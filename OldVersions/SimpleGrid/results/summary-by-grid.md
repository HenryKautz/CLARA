alone. "Is top": the runs in which the true goal alone has the highest
posterior. Examples (evidence files): 3×3 16, 4×4 36, 5×5 64, 6×6 100, 7×7 144,
8×8 196. Time limit per run: 1 minute (3×3, 4×4); 15 minutes (5×5, 6×6); 20
minutes (7×7, 8×8). Runs that did not finish (counted as misses, left out of the
mean times; the per-size tables say why): 7×7 sharpsat-td 36, mc-sat 2; 8×8

table says how many of the runs made finished): 8×8 sharpsat-td: 162; 8×8 mc-
